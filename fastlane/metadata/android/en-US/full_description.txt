Frequenza is a travel radio. Turn the dial and tune in to a live station somewhere in the world.

Every station is a stop on your trip:
• the map draws the route from where you started to the new station;
• you see how many kilometres you travelled and what time it is there;
• your latest stops stay on the map, and your trips are counted.

Save the stations you love to your favourites. The music keeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification.

No account, no ads. Stations come from the free Radio Browser directory.
