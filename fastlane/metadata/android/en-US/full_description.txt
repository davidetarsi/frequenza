Frequenza is a travel radio. Turn the dial and tune in to a live station somewhere in the world: a crackle of static, and you have landed in another country.

A TRIP WITH EVERY TURN
• the map draws the route from your last stop to the new station;
• you see how many kilometres you travelled and what time it is there;
• you read the title of the song on air, when the station sends it;
• your latest stops stay on the map, and your trips are counted.

THE STATIONS YOU LOVE
One tap on the heart saves a station to your favourites, newest first. Go back to the places you liked whenever you want.

LISTEN YOUR WAY
• the music keeps playing with the screen off;
• controls in the notification, on the lock screen and from your headphones;
• go back to the previous station or fly on to the next one;
• light or dark theme, in English or Italian.

SIMPLE AND FREE
No account, no ads, only usage statistics you can turn off. Stations come from the free Radio Browser directory, and the app's code is open on GitHub.
